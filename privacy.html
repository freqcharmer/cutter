Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026

Short version: FantastiCut doesn’t ask who you are, doesn’t track you, and doesn’t upload your designs.

1. What FantastiCut does not collect

FantastiCut has no accounts, no sign-up, no ads, and no analytics or tracking cookies. Your design files and cut jobs are processed in your browser on your own computer. The connection to your cutter runs directly between your browser and the cutter; that data never passes through our servers.

2. What is stored on your computer

FantastiCut may save your settings, such as material presets and your last-used options, in your browser’s local storage so they’re there next time. This stays on your device. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser’s site data for emfdprodco.com.

3. Server logs

FantastiCut is hosted by gHostilla. Like nearly all web servers, it automatically keeps standard access logs, which include your IP address, browser type, the pages requested, and the time. These logs are used only to keep the site running and secure. They are not sold or shared, except where required by law.

4. Third-party services

FantastiCut loads its typeface from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google’s servers when the page loads. Google’s own privacy policy covers that connection.

5. Email

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply to you.

6. Children

FantastiCut is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email contact@emfdprodco.com.
